HOW TO PLAY · RULES & TUTORIAL

Welcome to SKYBOUND RELICS! Master these basics before stepping into the portals:

1. MISSION & LEVEL RULES (400 LEVELS · 4 REALMS) • Reclaim the shattered Sky Crown across Sky Forest, Sky Beach, Sky Desert & Sky Dungeon.
• Sealed Sky Relic: You cannot rush past enemies! Defeat at least 65% of Guardians (and the Boss every 10th level) to break the red seal and finish each stage.
• Active Obstacles: Jump or Dash over Pop-Up Spikes, Spinning Blades, Swinging Axes, Sliding Crushers, Crumbling Bridges, Energy Turrets & Biome Hazards!
• Hearts & 1UP Heart Stones: Toggle Auto Heart Regen (+1 ♥ every 15s) anytime inside OPTION → GAME, or grab glowing 1UP ♥ Heart Stones placed across islands!
2. CONTROLS (KEYBOARD & MOUSE · CONTROLLER · MOBILE) • Keyboard & Mouse: Click the game to aim with 360° Mouse Look (Esc or Tab frees your cursor for menus). W/A/S/D Move & Strafe · Left-Click / F Attack · Right-Click / Shift Directional Dash · Space Jump / Double-Jump · T Talk to NPC · J Quest Log · Scroll Zoom.
• Custom Button Placement: Customize touch button positions anytime in OPTION → CONTROLS → EDIT BUTTON LAYOUT!
• Xbox / PlayStation Controller: Plug-and-play (Left Stick Move · Right Stick Cam · A/✕ Jump · X/□/RT Attack · B/○ Dash). Switch control modes anytime in OPTION → CONTROLS.
3. LOOT, 15-SLOT STASH & UPGRADE STORE • Enemies drop Credit Coins and rarely drop weapons. Picked-up weapons go straight to your 15-Slot Stash (your equipped weapon never switches automatically!).
• Open STORE anytime to buy Knives, Daggers, Bows, Elemental Arrows, Spears, Swords & Battle Axes, upgrade Weapons & 3D Armor up to Lv 6 Mythic Glow, or sell extra Stash items.
• Look for Hidden Golden Chests every 25th level & the Merchant Store every 10th level!
4. CLOUD SAFE HAVEN, GUILDS (100 MEMBERS) & SKY ARENA • Talk to Safe Haven NPCs (Elder Rowan, Merchant Mira, Blacksmith Torin, Guild Master Kael, Champion Rex) to unlock Story Quests & rewards.
• Create or join a Guild (up to 100 members) for permanent buffs — Warning: Guild Hard Mode makes enemies tougher & smarter, with bonus Arena rewards!
• Click ARENA (or enter the center portal) to battle tactical AI NPC Challengers across 4 Tiers!
